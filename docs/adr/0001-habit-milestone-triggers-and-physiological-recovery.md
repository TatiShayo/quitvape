# ADR 0001: Clinical Physiological Recovery Milestones and Relapse Invariants

## Context
Nicotine addiction recovery apps fail when progress metrics are mathematically arbitrary or demoralizing during minor slips.

## Decision
1. **Clinical Milestone Timeline**: Anchor recovery badges to verified medical recovery timeframes.
2. **Cumulative vs Streak Separation**: Preserve lifetime savings and badges while resetting the active streak.
3. **90-Second Emergency Interventions**: Fast sensory grounding tools for acute cravings.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: High user retention, medically defensible metrics, and compassionate behavioral reinforcement.
- **Negative**: Requires precise client-side streak calculation accounting for device time changes.
