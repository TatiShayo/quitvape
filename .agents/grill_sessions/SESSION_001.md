## Round 1: Core Architecture & Invariant Frontier

❓ **Q1** - **Physiological Recovery Math**: How do we calculate precise health milestones (e.g. blood oxygen normalization, carbon monoxide clearance, nicotine clearance) without medical misinformation?
➡️ *Recommendation*: Peer-reviewed physiological recovery timeline: 8 hours (CO clearance), 24 hours (heart risk drops), 72 hours (nicotine 100% metabolized).

**Architect Decision**: APPROVED. Grounding milestone triggers in clinical medical research provides genuine motivation and medical safety.

---

❓ **Q2** - **Craving Emergency Panic Button**: When a user experiences an acute nicotine craving spike, what is the instant intervention mechanism?
➡️ *Recommendation*: Immediate 90-second neuro-distraction loop (box breathing visualizer + emergency craving audio prompt).

**Architect Decision**: APPROVED. 90-second sensory interruption successfully breaks the dopamine craving loop.

## Round 2: Edge Cases & Failure Modes Frontier

❓ **Q3** - **Relapse Reset Invariants**: When a user records a slip/relapse, how is progress handled without demotivating the user?
➡️ *Recommendation*: Milestone preservation: Preserve total smoke-free days and money saved; reset the current active streak counter with a compassionate recovery prompt.

**Architect Decision**: APPROVED. Never wipe out a user's lifetime progress on a single slip; maintain cumulative health gains.
